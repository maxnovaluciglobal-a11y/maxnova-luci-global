## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is the operator's annual report: a cover that lists the operating companies, interior pages that file each holding with its facts and a real screen, a founder's letter, and a company-information back page. It refuses the agency template (hero claim + service cards + vague metrics + particles).
OWN-WORLD: Committed cobalt cover field (#1d34b8-ish) carrying paper-white type; interior on cool paper (#f3f4f2) with near-black ink, hairline rules at ink 15%, tabular figures, report schedules (rows, not cards). One family, Archivo variable: expanded heavy for headlines, normal width for text. Cobalt is the only colour besides each product's own logo. Squares as status marks.
STORY: Visitor sees in one glance that this company runs real software businesses, opens one to check, reads what they can do for them and how, meets the founder, confirms the legal entity, writes.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed cobalt cover. Top rule: monogram + wordmark left; Portfolio, Services, Company, EN/ES, and a paper "Talk to us" button right. Left 7 columns: H1 "We build and run software companies." at ~5.5rem expanded, subtitle beneath, primary "Talk to us" + text link "See the portfolio". Right 5 columns, bottom-aligned: the "Schedule of operating companies" table (company · sector · status), each row a link, live rows marked with a solid square. Mobile: H1, CTA, then the schedule beneath, still inside the cover.
FORM: Annual report (cover → schedules → letter → back page). Concept brief-pinned by Walter ("carta anual de un operador"); no concept-seed roll. OWN-WORLD (cobalt cover and back cover, cool paper, Archivo at two widths, schedule-table hero) approved by Walter on 2026-10-18 in his words: "Apruebo el look cobalto/Archivo tal como está".
